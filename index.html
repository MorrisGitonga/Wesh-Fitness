Meet Coach Aree

Morris Mutwiri Gitonga

I've spent over 8 years mastering karate — the discipline, the mindset, the technique. That same dedication now fuels everything I do at Wesh Fitness.

For the past 2 years, I've trained clients one-on-one at the gym, helping them build real strength and confidence, whether they're stepping into fitness for the first time or leveling up their conditioning. I also specialize in meal planning — designing practical, sustainable plans for both weight loss and weight gain, so nutrition works with your training, not against it.

My mission is simple: teach people to move well, defend themselves, and feel strong in their own bodies — inside the gym and out.

Wesh Fitness actually started as a TikTok series — breaking drills down for people who'll probably never set foot in a gym. This site is the next step for anyone who wants to go from watching @coacharee to training with him directly.
